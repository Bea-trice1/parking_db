Automated Rate Calculation
Mathematical formulation executed in stored procedure
Charge = CEIL(MAX(Minutes - Grace, 0) / 60) * HourlyRate + AssistFee

If assistance was provided by staff, fn_assist_fee automatically assesses a UGX 3,000 attendant surcharge.
